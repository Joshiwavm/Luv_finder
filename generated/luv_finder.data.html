luv_finder.data¶

Measurement-set I/O and visibility-domain operations.

Visibilities are held per (field, spectral window) in a Chunk: the weighted visibilities X = w * V and the weight of every visibility w (0 where flagged) as (n_chan, n_row) arrays, and what is constant along an axis (u, v, time, baseline and scan per row, frequency per channel) once. The weights are corrected on reading, per scan and channel, by the noise measured in the data (reweighted()).

Positions are offsets in one sky frame per dataset: arcsec east and north of Metadata.ref, the model’s (dra, ddec) convention. Each chunk carries its field’s phase centre in that frame, so the same (dra, ddec) means the same sky position in every pointing.

CASA (casatools) is imported lazily inside the functions that read a measurement set, so the NPZ path (DataHandler.from_npz()) works in an environment without CASA.

jackknife() → Chunk[source]¶

A signal-free noise realisation: consecutive integrations of every baseline differenced and halved.

Each baseline’s integrations are paired in time order, its first with its second, third with fourth, …, whatever the other baselines do, so a mosaic field seen once per visit (or a baseline missing from some integrations) still pairs nearly every row; an odd last integration is left out. A signal constant over a pair cancels; the noise stays, with weight pair_weight (2w for equal weights).

The split is by integration, never random: a random sign flip of individual visibilities destroys the baseline structure and does not give the observation’s white-noise level.

luv_finder.data.iter_chunks(msfile: str, ref, fields=None, spws=None, reweight: bool = True, scales: dict | None = None) → Iterator[Chunk][source]¶

Read target visibilities one spw at a time over all fields, weights corrected, offsets from ref.

Autocorrelations, flagged rows and rows with no unflagged channel are dropped. Every window’s weights are divided by the noise measured in it (reweighted()), so the peak memory is about twice one window over all its fields; reweight=False keeps the measurement set’s weights, for a noiseless simulation, which has no noise to measure. A scales dict receives each window’s WeightScale by spw.
