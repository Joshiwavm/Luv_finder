luv_finder.catalogue.catalogue(result: SearchResult, ref=None, floor: float = 4.0, k: float = 3.0, fidelity_min: float = 0.6, rule: str = 'and') → Table[source]¶

Catalogue the line candidates of a search: one row per group of the data, by S/N descending.

The noise correlation of result is measured, and the data, the jackknife and the negated data are grouped (groups()) on the filter’s S/N (see the module docstring). Each data group gets the likelihood ratio and the fidelity against the jackknife’s groups (likelihood(), fidelity()). A group passes the likelihood ratio if Lambda >= k or no jackknife group is as bright: there Lambda diverges, and its lower limit is the last finite value, at the brightest jackknife group. It is detected if it passes the likelihood ratio and its fidelity is at least fidelity_min (rule="and", the default; a fidelity that could not be fitted does not count against it), or either (rule="or"). Nothing is clipped: the candidates are cat[cat["detected"]].

Parameters:
resultSearchResult

PB-corrected (and, for a mosaic, combined) search with its jackknife.

ref(RA, Dec) in rad, optional

The sky frame’s reference (ref); adds ra and dec.

floorfloat

Lowest S/N of a local maximum, and the lower edge of the fidelity bins.

kfloat

Likelihood-ratio threshold.

fidelity_minfloat

Fidelity threshold.

rule{“or”, “and”}

How the two tests combine into detected.

Returns:
astropy.table.Table

Columns id, dra, ddec (arcsec), ra, dec (deg, with ref), freq_ghz, the template’s bmin, bmaj, pa and width, the snr, flux and error (Jy) and line_flux, line_flux_error (Jy km/s) at the brightest maximum, coverage, n_peaks, likelihood, likelihood_lower_limit, fidelity and detected. meta holds the jackknife_spread, the noise correlation’s hwhm_major, hwhm_minor, pa, spectral_hwhm and sidelobe, the thresholds, the jackknife’s noise tail (noise_amplitude, noise_sigma), the lowest data S/N with Lambda >= k (snr_likelihood) and with a fidelity of at least fidelity_min (snr_fidelity), NaN if none, the S/N of every group of the data, the jackknife and the negated data (snr_data, snr_jackknife, snr_negative) and the binned fidelity (fidelity_bins), all as lists and floats.

Raises:
ValueError

If result has no jackknife.

luv_finder.catalogue.fidelity(snr_data, snr_noise, floor: float, step: float = 0.25) → tuple[ndarray, tuple, dict][source]¶

Fidelity 1 - N_noise / N_data per S/N bin, N_noise from a Gaussian tail fitted to snr_noise.

The noise groups above floor are fitted, unbinned, with N(>= s) = A erfc(s / sqrt(2) sigma) (González-López et al. 2019), so the fidelity at high S/N comes from the tail and not from a few sparse bins, and an excess of data over noise near the floor leaves it alone. Every data group gets its bin’s fidelity. Returns those, (A, sigma) and the bins: the binned counts, their fidelity with its error and the model fidelity.

Above the brightest noise group the ratio diverges; those groups get its last finite value, N_data(>= that group) / 1, as a lower limit (flagged in the second array).
